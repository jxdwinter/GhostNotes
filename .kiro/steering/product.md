# GhostNotes Product

GhostNotes helps people retain what was said in meetings. It is for individuals and small teams who want a quick record of the conversation without manually writing minutes.

The core product flow is Record → Transcribe on-device → Generate structured minutes → Export. Structured minutes contain a summary, decisions, and action items with owners and optional due dates. The scaffold uses mock data so the flow is demonstrable without an account, network request, or API key.
